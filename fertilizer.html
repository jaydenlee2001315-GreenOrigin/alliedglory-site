Product Architecture · 产品架构

Complete System一瓶集成，全面覆盖

Macro and micro elements, plant-source organic matter, multi-dimensional amino acids, and a high-activity Bacillus subtilis culture, blended into a single ready-to-use formula — no complex mixing, no separate additive schedule.
